Our mission

Celebrating Black and mixed heritage with dignity, pride and belonging.

Black & Mixed Heritage exists to recognise the multiplicity of Black identities and mixed heritage across the world—from ancestral roots and the African diaspora to contemporary communities shaping the future through culture, creativity and collective care.

We centre DEEIA values—Diversity, Equity, Inclusion, Accessibility and belonging—through accessible design, respectful storytelling and community-led learning experiences that welcome everyone.
