About

Honouring ancestry. Amplifying voices. Building belonging.

Black & Mixed Heritage is a global portal celebrating Black and mixed cultures, African diaspora history, and the living traditions that continue to shape communities, identities and futures across the world.

Our mission

Celebrating Black and mixed heritage with dignity, pride and belonging.

We exist to recognise the multiplicity of Black identities and mixed heritage across the world—from ancestral roots and the African diaspora to contemporary communities shaping the future through culture, creativity and collective care.

Through accessible storytelling, community learning and inclusive design, we create a space where heritage is shared with respect, joy and visibility.
