User guide

How to use the hub

The hub is one page with six menus. Each menu has its own colour, so you can tell where you are. Take a guided tour, follow a step-by-step guide, or jump straight to a menu.

Guided tours

Take a tour

Each tour moves through the hub one step at a time and highlights what to look at. Use Back and Next, or press Escape to stop at any time.
