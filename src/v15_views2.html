Inclusive iconography

Iconography bank

People icons in any of the ten Monk skin tones, a mixed group, or no skin tone at all, plus culture and signposting icons used across the hub. Copy any icon as SVG.

Use people icons well

  • Show a range of tones across a page, not one "default".
  • Use "no skin tone" when a person's identity is not the point.
  • Never use skin tone alone to carry meaning; label it in words.
  • Write alt text about the role or action ("volunteer", "speaker"), not appearance, unless appearance matters.

What we avoid

  • Exaggerated features, "tribal" clichés or costume.
  • Pairing darker tones with poverty, crime or need.
  • Sacred or ceremonial symbols as decoration.
  • Yellow "neutral" figures that quietly default to white.
