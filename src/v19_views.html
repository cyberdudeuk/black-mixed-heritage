A proposal for comment

Help run the hub all year round

Black history is not only for October. This proposal sets out how a small group of volunteers could run, maintain and grow the hub through the year, alongside existing networks. Comment on it, suggest changes, or say you would like to help.

Why

One person built the hub. To stay accurate, accessible and useful it needs shared ownership, regular checks and people from the communities it describes.

What we propose

Thirteen roles at four levels of commitment, a monthly, quarterly and annual rhythm, and clear ways to suggest ideas and report problems.

How to respond

Leave a comment on this page, add to the ideas box below, or send an expression of interest. Nothing is decided yet.
