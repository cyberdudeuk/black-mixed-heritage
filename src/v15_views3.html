Everyone belongs here

Mixed heritage and belonging

Many people have more than one heritage. Someone of mixed heritage may identify fully, partly or not at all with Black history, and that can change over a lifetime. This hub is for all of them: no one has to prove how Black they are to take part, and no one should be told which part of their family history counts.

Sharing culture well

Cultural appreciation and cultural appropriation

Celebrating, learning about and taking part in other people's heritage is welcome when it is done with consent, respect and credit, and not for profit. Cultural appropriation usually means taking elements of a culture without understanding, credit or permission, especially where there is an imbalance of power or someone profits.
