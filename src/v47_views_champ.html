Champion pack

Champions and ambassadors help the hub reach colleagues in every department, organisation, profession and staff network. It takes an hour or two a month, and you need no special knowledge. Everything here is ready to use.

  1. Share it where people look: post one of the texts below on your intranet, in a team channel or in your network's newsletter.
  2. Add it to Teams: add the hub as a tab in your team's channel (see Teams and GitHub Pages (opens in a new tab)), or ask your Teams administrator to publish it for everyone.
  3. Put up a poster: make one on the Posters page; each carries a QR code to the hub.
  4. Subscribe your team: add the calendar to a shared calendar, and follow what is new in a feed reader.
  5. Pass things on: send colleagues' ideas, corrections and events through the forms on this page.
  6. Each quarter: post a short highlight in the repository's Discussions, or in your own network.

To become a champion or join a field review panel, use Express interest; outside Claude it opens the public volunteer form.
