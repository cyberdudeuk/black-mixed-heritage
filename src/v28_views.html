Two words that are often confused

Race and ethnicity: what each means and which to use

People often say “race” when they mean ethnicity. The two are not the same, and UK law, official statistics and science each use them in a particular way. This page explains the difference and the rule this hub follows. Every point links to its source.

Not interchangeable

Why “Black” and “African” are not the same thing

Not every Black person is African, and not every African is Black. Using one word for the other erases Caribbean, Black British and other Black identities, and the Arab, Amazigh and other peoples of Africa.
