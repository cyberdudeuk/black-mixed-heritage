Routes in and up, for the people this hub is for

Every route here exists today or is in the proposal above. Joining government, If you are treated unfairly and Taking part in public life have the detail on each.

  • Young people, including those not in education, employment or training, and care leavers: work experience and holiday placements in every location, the Career Launch Apprenticeship, apprenticeships from level 2, the Care Leavers Internship Scheme.
  • People without a degree, returners and career changers: TechTrack (level 4), apprenticeships up to level 7, the Cyber Security Academy (no technical qualifications needed), the Digital and Data Fast Stream route for serving civil servants.
  • People with convictions, veterans and carers: Going Forward into Employment, with the universal layer from the first day.
  • Black, African, Caribbean and Mixed-heritage civil servants: every route above, with a sponsor and mentor built in, and progression counted by group.
  • Leaders: digital and data leadership, and leading inclusive organisations, for every profession.
