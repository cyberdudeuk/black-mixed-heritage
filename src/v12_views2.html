Brand and media hub

The hub's identity

An original identity for public sector use in 2026. It is not the Black History Month UK logo; credit the national theme to Black History Month UK wherever you use it.

The mark: three linked rings

Gold, red and green rings, linked like the Nkonsonkonson chain: people joined in community. Keep clear space of one ring's width around it, and never stretch, rotate or recolour it.

Tone of voice

  • Warm and joyful for celebration: food, music, achievement, invitations.
  • Quiet and respectful for remembrance: slavery, racist violence, loss.
  • Plain and specific always: short sentences, real names, dates and sources.
  • Never mix jokes into remembrance, use stereotypes, or speak for a community.

Using the kit

Do and don't

Do

  • Add your organisation's name in the poster settings.
  • Keep the alternative text when you reuse an image online.
  • Use real photos of your own colleagues only with their consent.
  • Credit the national theme to Black History Month UK.

Don't

  • Use stock images of anonymous Black people as decoration.
  • Add motifs whose meaning you don't know.
  • Put celebration graphics on remembrance content.
  • Use the kit to promote a product, party or campaign.
