Annual programme

A year-round celebration of African-rooted heritage.

We are building a calendar of remembrance, celebration, learning and action that keeps Black and mixed heritage visible across every season.

January

Roots & ancestry

Family history, genealogies, oral traditions and the importance of knowing where we come from.

February

Women & leadership

Honouring Black women’s contributions in culture, activism, science, faith and community life.

March

Education & learning

School and community learning programmes that bridge history, identity and belonging.

April

Creative expression

Art, performance, music, spoken word and visual culture as channels for lived experience.

May

Migration & belonging

Stories of movement, adaptation and new home-making across communities and continents.

June

Pan-African connection

Exploring African unity, regional relationships and the links between heritage and identity.

July

Heritage & culture

Celebrating language, culinary practices, dress, ritual, dance and intergenerational memory.

August

Youth voices

Highlighting young people shaping identity, activism and creative futures.

September

Community action

Solidarity, local organising, civic engagement and collective community care.

October

Black History Month

The UK and global spotlight on Black history, achievement and cultural visibility.

November

Global diaspora stories

Listening to transnational voices and exploring the many geographies of the Black Atlantic.

December

Joy, reflection & renewal

Celebration, gratitude, family heritage and planning for the year ahead.

Why this matters

Annual planning turns heritage into action.

A year-round programme ensures heritage work is not reduced to a single month. It creates continuity in education, cultural participation, story-sharing and community pride.

This annual rhythm connects the UK Black History Month to a wider global movement of remembrance, representation and future-building.
