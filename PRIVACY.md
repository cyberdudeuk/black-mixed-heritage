# Privacy

## The website
- The hub collects no personal data and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
- Preferences and progress (theme, Access settings, the sections you have seen, checklists, poster fields, ideas you keep locally) are kept in your own browser (local storage) and never leave it. Clearing your browser data removes them.
- The ideas list on the public site is a file of issue titles, labels and counts published from this repository. It holds no issue text and no usernames.
- The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts and two small libraries (QR code and barcode) from cdnjs. Like any web request, these services see your IP address; the hub sends them nothing else. Links to other sites are covered by those sites' own policies.

## Inside Claude
When the hub is opened inside Claude, book and film club votes, ideas and expressions of interest can be saved in the page's shared store under an opaque ID, not a name. Expressions of interest are readable only by the hub's owner. You can withdraw yours at any time.

## GitHub
Issues, comments and pull requests are public and are covered by GitHub's privacy statement. An automated check labels issues that seem to contain an email address, phone number or National Insurance number as `needs-redaction`; such issues are kept off the hub until a maintainer has removed the data.

## Questions
Open an issue (without personal details), or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for anything sensitive.
