# Help run the Black History and Heritage Hub all year round

*A proposal for comment. Version 0.1, 21 October 2026. Nothing here is decided.*

## Summary

The hub was built by one person. To stay accurate, accessible and useful beyond October, it needs shared ownership, regular checks and people from the communities it describes. This proposal suggests 13 volunteer roles (about 32 people, 72 to 117 hours a month in total), a monthly, quarterly and annual rhythm, and clear ways for anyone to suggest ideas or report problems.

## What we are asking

1. **Comment** on the roles, commitment and programme below.
2. **Tell us** what your network already does, so the hub supports it rather than duplicates it.
3. **Express interest** in a role, using the form on the hub's "Help run the hub" page.

## Roles, profiles and commitment

| Role | People | Level | Hours each | Profile | What they do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hub owner | 1 | Core | 16 to 24 a month | Experienced in content or digital work; can make final calls. | Sets priorities and approves releases; Chairs the quarterly review; Final say on corrections and complaints |
| Deputy owner | 1 | Regular | 6 to 8 a month | Can step in when the owner is away; removes the single point of failure. | Covers the owner; Runs monthly triage of ideas and issues; Keeps the change log |
| Page stewards | 4 | Regular | 2 to 4 a month | One for each menu: Learn, Act, Make and share, Reference. | Keep their pages current; Act on comments and corrections; Propose improvements |
| Evidence checkers | 4 | Light | 2 to 3 a month | Careful readers; analysts and librarians welcome. | Re-read sources flagged as stale; Check new figures before release; Record what was checked and when |
| Accessibility champions | 2 | Light | 4 to 6 a quarter | Use or test with assistive technology. | Quarterly accessibility check; Test new features with a screen reader and keyboard; Recruit testers |
| Community reviewers | 6 | Light | 2 to 4 a quarter | Members of the communities the hub describes, across nations and heritages. | Review motifs, words, proverbs and recipes; Flag anything inaccurate or hurtful; Suggest people and stories to add |
| Data and dashboards lead | 1 | Regular | 3 to 4 a month | Analyst or statistician. | Refresh figures when new statistics are published; Keep dashboards and benchmarks current; Check charts and tables |
| Maintainers | 2 | Regular | 3 to 6 a month | Comfortable with HTML, JavaScript and GitHub. | Triage bugs on GitHub; Review pull requests; Run the tests and publish releases |
| Events and outreach coordinators | 4 | Regular | 3 to 5 a month | Network members who organise events. | Link network events to the calendar; Run club meetings and talks; Collect feedback after events |
| Nation leads | 3 | Light | 3 to 4 a quarter | One each for Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. | Make sure content reflects their nation; Connect devolved networks; Review curriculum and law entries |
| Learning curators | 2 | Light | 2 to 3 a term | Teachers, learning designers or parents. | Refresh learning resources each term; Check links and age ranges; Add new quiz questions from sourced content |
| Communications partner | 1 | Light | 2 to 3 a month | Internal communications colleague. | Promote monthly highlights; Share posters and Teams backgrounds; Keep messages accessible |
| Senior sponsor | 1 | Sponsor | 1 to 2 a quarter | Senior leader who champions the hub. | Attends the quarterly review; Removes blockers; Supports time for volunteers |

Commitment levels: **Core** (about 2 to 3 days a month), **Regular** (about 2 to 8 hours a month), **Light** (a few hours a month or a quarter), **Sponsor** (about an hour or two a quarter).

**Capacity:** about 32 people giving 72 to 117 hours a month in total, roughly 10 to 16 working days. Two people in core roles (owner and deputy) so the hub never depends on one person. These are planning estimates, not commitments.

**Rules on network time:** staff network activity must follow your organisation's rules. For the Civil Service, see: Cabinet Office: Civil Service staff networks to meet outside working hours (https://www.gov.uk/government/news/civil-service-staff-networks-to-only-meet-outside-working-hours-and-have-all-events-signed-off-by-senior-managers).

## Regular rhythm

|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | Ideas and issues triage | Deputy owner, maintainers | Sort new ideas, bugs and corrections; agree what goes into the next release |
| Monthly | Freshness check | Page stewards, evidence checkers | Run the staleness routine; re-read any source older than 12 months |
| Monthly | Book and film club | Events coordinators, club hosts | Monthly meeting, chosen by vote |
| Monthly | Monthly highlight | Communications partner | One story, one fact and one action, shared across networks |
| Monthly | Release | Maintainers | Publish fixes with tests and a change-log entry |
| Quarterly | Quarterly review | Owner, sponsor, stewards | Check progress against the theory of change and agree priorities |
| Quarterly | Accessibility check | Accessibility champions | Manual check of key journeys with assistive technology |
| Quarterly | Community review | Community reviewers | Review new and changed content |
| Quarterly | Data refresh | Data lead | Update dashboards and benchmarks with new official releases |
| Quarterly | Nations sync | Nation leads | Review coverage for Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland |
| Quarterly | User research round | Owner, volunteers | Five sessions with different personas |
| Annual (January) | Annual plan | Owner, sponsor | Set priorities for the year and confirm roles |
| Annual (Spring and autumn) | Award nominations cycle | Owner, stewards | Nominate colleagues and the hub where it fits |
| Annual (October) | Black History Month season | Everyone | Full calendar, theme content, posters, events |
| Annual (November and December) | Annual review and release | Owner, maintainers | Independent-style review, big release, thank-you to volunteers |

## Working with existing communities and networks

| Partner | What we do together | Their role and ours |
|---|---|---|
| Staff networks and race forums | Co-plan events; share the calendar; use posters and templates | Lead events; the hub promotes and records them |
| Cross-government networks | Share good practice and data across departments | Contribute content and reviewers |
| Professional and academic networks | Talks, research links and mentoring | Inform content; host club and learning sessions |
| Community organisations and archives | Stories, oral histories, local heritage | Review content; co-host events with consent and credit |
| Schools, colleges and educators | Learning resources, games, family activities | Curate resources each term |
| Devolved governments and nation networks | Nation-specific law, curriculum and events | Nation leads keep content current |
| Communications teams | Monthly highlights and October campaign | Share accessible materials |

The hub supports networks' own programmes; it does not replace them. Networks keep ownership of their events and decide what to share.

## Ideas, wishlist, bugs and corrections

- On the hub: an ideas box where anyone can suggest ideas, wish for features, report bugs, accessibility barriers or wrong facts, and support others' suggestions.
- On GitHub: issue forms for bugs, corrections, accessibility, ideas and features in the public repository cyberdudeuk/black-mixed-heritage. Issues are public, so no personal information.
- Monthly triage decides what goes into the next release; every fixed defect gets a test.

## Governance

- **Owner** makes final decisions; **deputy** covers.
- **Quarterly review** with the senior sponsor checks progress against the theory of change.
- **Corrections** are made, checked and logged; the self-checks are updated so the same error cannot return.
- **Privacy:** expressions of interest are visible only to the owner and can be withdrawn at any time.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Volunteers drift away after October | Light roles, a clear rhythm, thanks and recognition |
| Content goes out of date | Monthly freshness check and staleness routine |
| Key-person dependency | Owner and deputy; documented build and tests on GitHub |
| Inaccurate or hurtful content | Community reviewers and a correction route |
| Network time rules | Plan within each organisation's rules; senior sponsor support |
