# Using the hub

- **On the web:** https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/black-mixed-heritage/ works on any device.
- **Offline:** download `bhm-2026-hub.html` from the [latest release](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/black-mixed-heritage/releases/latest) and open it in a browser.
- **In Microsoft Teams:** add the Teams app from the release as a tab.
- **Find your way:** eight menus (This month, Lead, Careers, Practice, Learn, Understand, Resources, About), **Search** (press `/`), and guided tours under **About → User guide and tours**.
- **Accessibility:** text size, a highly legible font, spacing, high contrast and motion settings under **Access** at the top right; full keyboard use.
- **Calendar:** subscribe to `https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/black-mixed-heritage/data/bhm2026-events.ics`.

Full guide: the [user manual](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/black-mixed-heritage/blob/main/docs/user-manual.md).
