# BHM 2026 Hub

A hub celebrating Black and Black-Mixed history, heritage and cultures, Africa and the African diaspora, built for Black History Month 2026 in the UK and designed to run all year. Every figure links to a source that was read.

**[Open the hub](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/black-mixed-heritage/)** · [Download the single file](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/black-mixed-heritage/releases/latest) · [Report a problem](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/black-mixed-heritage/issues/new/choose)

Made in a personal capacity. Unofficial: not published or endorsed by Black History Month UK or HM Government.

This wiki is a short front door. The full documentation lives in the repository's [docs folder](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/black-mixed-heritage/tree/main/docs) and is reviewed like everything else. The wiki is generated from `docs/wiki/`, so please suggest changes by pull request; edits made here are overwritten.
