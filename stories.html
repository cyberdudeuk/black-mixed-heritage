About us

Celebrating complexity, community and belonging.

Black & Mixed Heritage is a portal for reflection, education and connection—designed to honour the rich diversity of Black and mixed cultural identities across the world.

Our purpose

To create a world where identity is not flattened, but expressed with pride.

We believe heritage should be accessible, honest and expansive. Our work centres the lived experiences of Black and mixed communities, recognising both the strengths of cultural continuity and the realities of displacement, racism, exclusion and erasure.

By sharing stories, preserving memory and promoting inclusive learning, we help people understand how the African diaspora, global migration and cultural exchange have shaped modern life around the world.

Our principles

DEEIA-centred design and community practice

D

Diversity

We honour varied histories, identities, languages and traditions without treating Blackness or mixed heritage as monolithic.

E

Equity

We centre fairness, representation and access so communities are visible and heard in the stories we tell.

I

Inclusion

We create welcoming spaces for dialogue across generations, geographies and lived experiences.

A

Accessibility

We design with clarity, readability, usability and accessibility at every stage to make knowledge open to all.

B

Belonging

We create respectful communities where people can connect, learn and be fully present without pressure to assimilate.

✦

Care

We approach cultural stories with empathy, trauma-awareness and a commitment to dignity.
